About the Server

Our Community

ERLC Command Hub is the central platform for managing and coordinating our Emergency Response: Liberty County community.

Our goal is to provide an organised, professional and enjoyable roleplay environment where emergency services can operate together effectively.

This hub provides members and staff with access to important server information, departments, applications, reports and live operational tools.
